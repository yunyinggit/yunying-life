Circle Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 August 2026 · Operator: Yunying Ma

Circle is a personal health companion for iPhone. Circle does not sell personal data and contains no advertising or cross-app tracking. Raw Apple Health data, on-device health analysis, and original audio remain on your iPhone. Text notes, completed voice transcripts, and Health Agent conversations are synchronized to Circle's server to provide note and conversation features.

Information Circle processes

  • Apple Health data. With your permission, Circle reads information such as heart rate, beat-to-beat samples, ECG, heart-rate variability, activity, and sleep to create on-device trends. Raw Apple Health data is not uploaded to Circle's server.
  • Health notes. Typed notes and completed speech transcripts are stored under your Circle account and synchronized to the server. Original recordings stay on the iPhone where they were created and are not uploaded.
  • Health Agent conversations. Prompts, conversation history, and responses are retained so conversations can continue. Relevant conversation content and text notes may be sent to a third-party AI service provider to generate responses, but only after the user is informed and gives permission.
  • Account and connection data. Circle uses an administrator-issued username and password. The password is stored in the iOS Keychain, and server data is separated by account.
  • Technical data. The server may process limited request information needed for security and troubleshooting, such as timestamps, IP addresses, and error records. Circle does not use advertising SDKs or third-party behavioral analytics.

How information is used

Circle processes information only to provide its core features: on-device health trends, medication reminders, note storage and synchronization, ongoing Health Agent conversations, account security, and service troubleshooting. Apple Health data is not used for advertising, marketing, user profiling, or sale to data brokers.

Storage and service providers

Raw Apple Health data, on-device analysis, sleep diary entries, and original recordings are stored on your iPhone. Text notes, completed transcripts, and Agent conversations are stored on Circle's access-controlled server. Circle may use Apple's system services for health authorization and speech transcription, and a third-party AI service provider to process Health Agent requests. Those providers process relevant data under their own terms and privacy policies and are expected to provide protections consistent with this policy and applicable law.

Retention and deletion

On-device information remains until you delete it in the app or system, or uninstall the app. Server notes and conversations remain until the related account is removed or you request deletion. You may request access, correction, or deletion using the email below; we will verify the account before acting.

Your choices

You can withdraw Apple Health, microphone, speech recognition, and notification permissions in iOS Settings. You can decline third-party AI processing and continue to use Circle's on-device health, sleep, medication, and local note features. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict processing of, or obtain a copy of your personal data.

Security

Circle uses HTTPS for server communications, account-based access controls, and the iOS Keychain for passwords. No system can guarantee absolute security. We will take reasonable steps to address security incidents and provide notice when required by law.

Children and medical disclaimer

Circle is not directed to children under 13. Circle provides personal recordkeeping and general health information. It does not diagnose conditions and is not a substitute for a physician, pharmacist, or emergency service.

Changes and contact

If Circle's data practices materially change, this page and its date will be updated. For privacy questions or data requests, contact 939844441@qq.com.

Circle 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 8 月 24 日 · 运营者：Yunying Ma

Circle 不出售个人数据，不含广告或跨 App 跟踪。Apple 健康原始数据、本地健康分析、睡眠日记和原始录音保留在你的 iPhone 上；文字随记、完成后的语音转写以及健康 Agent 对话会同步到 Circle 服务器，以提供记录和对话功能。

Circle 处理哪些信息

  • Apple 健康数据：经你授权后，Circle 会读取心率、逐次心搏、心电图、心率变异性、活动和睡眠等数据，并在设备本地生成趋势。Apple 健康原始数据不会上传到 Circle 服务器。
  • 健康随记：文字随记和完成后的语音转写会保存到 Circle 账号并同步到服务器。原始录音只留在创建它的 iPhone 上。
  • 健康 Agent 对话：提问、对话历史和回复会保存在服务器上。相关对话和文字随记仅会在明确告知并取得同意后发送给第三方 AI 服务商，用于生成回复。
  • 账号和技术信息：密码保存在 iOS 钥匙串中；服务器按账号隔离数据，并可能处理安全与故障排查所需的时间、IP 地址和错误记录。Circle 不使用广告 SDK 或第三方行为分析。

用途、存储与第三方

信息仅用于本地健康趋势、用药提醒、随记同步、健康问答、账号安全和故障排查。Apple 健康数据不会用于广告、营销、用户画像或出售。Circle 可能使用 Apple 的系统服务完成健康授权和语音转写，并使用第三方 AI 服务商处理用户同意的 Agent 请求。

保留、删除与选择

设备信息会保留到你删除或卸载 App；服务器随记和对话会保留到账号被删除或你提出删除请求。你可以在 iOS 设置中撤回健康、麦克风、语音识别和通知权限，也可以拒绝第三方 AI 处理并继续使用本地健康、睡眠、用药和本地随记功能。

安全、儿童与医疗说明

Circle 使用 HTTPS、账号访问控制和 iOS 钥匙串保护信息。Circle 不面向 13 岁以下儿童。Circle 只提供个人记录和一般健康信息，不进行诊断，也不能替代医生、药师或紧急医疗服务。
